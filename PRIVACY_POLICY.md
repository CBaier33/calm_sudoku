# Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 08-09-2026

## 1. Introduction
Calm Sudoku (the "App"), developed by Christopher Baier ("I," "me," or "my"), is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how I handle your information when you use this mobile application on Android via Google Play.

## 2. Information We Collect
I do not collect, store, or share any personal information from you. The App is designed to function entirely without requiring access to your device's data or personal identifiers.

Specifically:
- I do not collect personal information (such as your name, email address, or phone number).
- I do not collect usage data, game scores, or analytics.
- I do not collect device information (such as your unique device ID, advertising ID, or hardware model).
- I do not collect location data.

## 3. App Permissions
This App does not request or require any permissions to access your device's hardware or software features. The App functions entirely locally on your device without accessing the camera, microphone, contacts, storage, or internet.

## 4. Third-Party Services
The App does not contain any third-party SDKs, analytics tools, advertising networks, or social media plugins. Consequently, no data is collected, processed, or shared with any third parties.

## 5. Data Security
Since I do not collect or store any personal information or game data, there is no data to secure or transmit over the network. All game logic runs locally on your device.

## 6. Children's Privacy
The App does not knowingly collect any personal information from children under the age of 13. Since no data is collected at all, no information is gathered from children.

## 7. Changes to This Privacy Policy
I may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If I make changes, I will notify you by revising the "Last Updated" date in this policy.
